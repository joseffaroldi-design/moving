# Southern Magnolia Movers — Local SEO Owner Checklist

## Google Business Profile

- Use the exact real-world business name: Southern Magnolia Movers.
- Use one profile for the service-area business unless there are genuinely separate staffed locations.
- Hide the business address if customers are not served there.
- Add only accurate cities/ZIPs actually served.
- Confirm the primary moving-company category and only relevant secondary categories.
- Confirm phone, production website, and business hours.
- Add each real moving service offered.
- Add current crew, truck, packing, and completed-move photos regularly.
- Use a factual business description without promotions or keyword stuffing.

## Reviews

- Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review after the move.
- Do not script keywords or offer incentives.
- Reply to reviews naturally and professionally.

## Search engines and maps

- Verify Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Claim/update Bing Places.
- Claim/update Apple Business Connect.
- Keep business name, phone, website, and service-area facts consistent across legitimate directories.

## Local authority

Prioritize real local relationships over bulk directory submissions:

- Realtors and brokers
- Apartment communities
- Storage facilities
- Property managers
- Local chambers
- Neighborhood organizations
- Senior relocation partners
- Estate-sale companies
- Local business associations

## Content expansion gate

Before adding a new city page, confirm that the company truly serves and wants leads from that market. The next candidates identified in the code audit are Covington, Mandeville, Slidell, Hammond, Gretna, Laplace, and Houma.
