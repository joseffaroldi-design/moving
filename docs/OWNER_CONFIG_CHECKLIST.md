# Southern Magnolia Movers — Owner/Operator Configuration Checklist

Complete these once before going live. Routes are relative to your app URL.
Never share passwords, API keys, JWTs, or the Supabase service-role key.

## 1. Production owner Auth account
- Supabase → Authentication → Users → confirm your real owner email exists with a password you know.
- Expected: you can sign in at `/login` and land on `/dashboard`.

## 2. Owner profile role + company
- After signing in, open **Settings** (`/dashboard/settings`) → **Account** card → **Role** should read **Owner**, and **Company** should show your company name.
- If Role shows "—", your profile role/company_id isn't set (owner-run Supabase check needed — ask the builder for the read-only query).

## 3. Business Profile  (`/dashboard/settings` → Business Profile card)
Fill and **Save** (`save-business-profile`). Available fields today:
- Business name (required) · Phone · Email · Address · Website
- Primary tagline · Secondary tagline
- **Default tax rate (%)** · **Default deposit (%)**
Expected: "Business profile saved" toast; the amber "Using defaults — not saved yet" chip disappears.

> Not yet configurable in the UI (currently brand defaults; convey manually or request as a future enhancement): **logo image, brand colors, quote terms, invoice terms, cancellation policy, payment instructions**. Quote/invoice PDFs currently show the built-in Southern Magnolia branding + phone/email.

## 4. Staff accounts + roles
- Roles supported: owner, operations_manager, dispatcher, sales, crew_lead, mover, customer.
- There is **no in-app "add staff" screen yet.** New staff sign up at `/login` (creates a base profile), then an owner sets their role/company in Supabase (owner-run). Request the exact steps from the builder if needed.

## 5. Disable/label test accounts
- Remove or clearly rename demo/test accounts before launch: `qa-owner@example.com`, `test-customer@example.com`, `smagnoliamoving@gmail.com` (if not your real login).
- Supabase → Authentication → Users → delete or keep clearly named as test.

## 6–7. Quote & invoice numbering
- Auto-generated by the system (`quote_number` / `invoice_number`) — no setup required. Confirm the format looks right on your first created quote/invoice.

## 8. Tax & deposit defaults
- Set in Business Profile (step 3). These prefill new quotes; you can still override per quote.

## 9. Email sender configuration
- **Important:** the app does **not** send customer emails yet. "Send quote"/"Send invoice" only change status in-app and expose the item in the Customer Portal. You currently notify customers by phone or by sharing the portal.
- Supabase Auth transactional emails (password reset, etc.) use Supabase's email settings → Authentication → Emails / SMTP. Configure a custom SMTP sender for production reliability.

## 10. Production domain
- See the Production Deployment Plan. Domain/SSL are configured in the Emergent deploy + your registrar.

## 11. Database backups
- Supabase → Database → Backups. On paid plans enable daily backups / PITR. Confirm a backup exists before launch.

## 12. Monitoring / logs
- Supabase → Logs (Postgres, Auth, Edge Functions). Emergent deploy logs via the deploy dashboard.

## 13. Customer-facing contact details
- Confirm the phone/email in Business Profile match what customers should use. These appear on the portal and PDFs.

## 14. Print/PDF branding
- Create one test quote + invoice and open Print/PDF; confirm the header, phone, email, totals, and deposit look correct.

## 15. Add/deactivate staff safely
- Until an in-app staff manager exists, do this in Supabase (owner-run): set/clear a profile's `role` and `is_active`. Deactivating (`is_active=false`) blocks their access without deleting history.
