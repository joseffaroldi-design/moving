# Southern Magnolia Movers — Owner/Operator Configuration Checklist

Complete these once before going live. Routes are relative to your app URL.
Never share passwords, API keys, JWTs, or the Supabase service-role key.

## 1. Production owner Auth account
- Supabase → Authentication → Users → confirm your real owner email exists with a password you know.
- Expected: you can sign in at `/login` and land on `/dashboard`.

## 2. Owner profile role + company
- After signing in, open **Settings** (`/dashboard/settings`) → **Account** card → **Role** should read **Owner**, and **Company** should show your company name.
- If Role shows "—", the profile needs to be attached to the company before launch.

## 3. Business Profile (`/dashboard/settings`)
Fill and **Save** (`save-business-profile`). The Settings screen now supports:
- Business name · phone · email · address · website · logo URL
- Primary and secondary taglines
- Primary, accent, and cream brand colors
- Default tax rate and default deposit percentage
- Quote terms · invoice terms · cancellation policy · payment instructions

Expected: "Business profile saved" toast; the amber "Using defaults — not saved yet" chip disappears.

## 4. Staff accounts + roles
- Open **Settings → Staff Access**.
- Owners and operations managers can invite staff by email, choose a role, change roles, and activate/deactivate accounts without opening the database console.
- Supported staff roles: owner, operations_manager, dispatcher, sales, crew_lead, mover.
- Operations managers cannot assign/remove the owner role or deactivate an owner. Nobody can change their own active status from the Staff Access panel.
- Invitations are sent through Supabase Auth, so configure a production SMTP sender before relying on staff invite email delivery at scale.

## 5. Test accounts
- Before launch, review Supabase → Authentication → Users and remove or clearly label demo/test accounts that are no longer needed.

## 6–7. Quote & invoice numbering
- Auto-generated by the system (`quote_number` / `invoice_number`) — no setup required. Confirm the format looks right on your first created quote/invoice.

## 8. Tax & deposit defaults
- Set in Business Profile (step 3). These prefill new quotes; you can still override per quote.

## 9. Email sender configuration
- Staff invitation and Supabase Auth transactional emails depend on Supabase email settings → Authentication → Emails / SMTP.
- Configure a custom SMTP sender for production reliability.
- Customer quote/invoice delivery is currently portal-first: marking an item sent exposes it in the Customer Portal. Staff can also share the portal link or notify the customer directly.

## 10. Production domain
- Domain/SSL are configured in the hosting/deploy platform and your registrar.

## 11. Database backups
- Supabase → Database → Backups. On paid plans enable daily backups / PITR. Confirm a backup exists before launch.

## 12. Monitoring / logs
- Supabase → Logs (Postgres, Auth, Edge Functions). Hosting deploy logs are available through the deployment dashboard.

## 13. Customer-facing contact details
- Confirm the phone/email in Business Profile match what customers should use. These appear across customer-facing surfaces and documents where supported.

## 14. Print/PDF branding
- Create one test quote + invoice and open Print/PDF; confirm the header, phone, email, totals, terms, and deposit look correct.

## 15. Staff access verification
- Invite one non-owner test staff account from **Settings → Staff Access**.
- Accept the invitation, sign in, confirm the expected role-specific navigation, then deactivate that test account from Settings.
